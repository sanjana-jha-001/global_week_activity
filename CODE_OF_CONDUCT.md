# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone. Be respectful, patient, and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.

## Expected behavior
- Be kind and respectful to others.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
Report concerns to the project maintainer at @sanjana-jha-001. Reports will be reviewed and handled fairly and confidentially.
